# ADR 0010: Stone equipment at level one and tool-independent passive gathering XP

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

- Stone tools and custom stone weapons require level 1 instead of level 5.
  Crafting continues to require Smithing; use continues to require the item's
  gameplay skill (Mining, Woodcutting, Digging, Farming, Light Weapons, or
  Heavy Weapons).
- Passive gathering XP is awarded for supported natural sources independently
  of the held tool, including Farming sources broken by hand. Player-placed
  blocks and creative/spectator actions remain ineligible.
- Active abilities keep their existing tool requirements: Vein Mining, Tree
  Feller, Field Harvest, and other active perk effects still require their
  respective tools.

## Consequences

The first stone tier becomes an early-progression milestone, while players can
start levelling from natural world interaction before receiving the correct
tool. This does not create synthetic drops, bypass material requirements, or
enable active gathering abilities by hand.
