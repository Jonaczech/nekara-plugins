# ADR 0006: Equipment proficiency requirements

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Crafting requirements remain owned by Smithing. Separately, material tiers now
require the relevant gameplay skill to use equipment at full efficiency. An
unqualified item is never deleted or hard-blocked: armor applies a movement and
protection penalty, while held weapons and tools apply movement, damage, and
block-breaking penalties. The player receives an action-bar explanation once
per currently unmet requirement.

Armor maps leather, chainmail, and diamond to Light Armor; copper, gold, iron,
and netherite map to Heavy Armor. Swords, daggers, and spears use Light Weapons;
greatswords and hammers use Heavy Weapons. Pickaxes, shovels, and hoes use
Mining, Digging, and Farming respectively.

Axes are hybrid equipment. Woodcutting controls their movement and harvesting
proficiency, while Heavy Weapons controls combat damage. This avoids forcing a
woodcutter to train combat merely to chop wood, without making an untrained
fighter's axe a full-strength weapon.
